Phonon voices

The voices of the Phonon TTS integration for Gladys Assistant. Listen to a sample, then pick the voice in the integration's configuration, under the name shown at the bottom of its card.

    Voices from the gradium/phonon-voices catalog (Apache 2.0), by Gradium. Every voice works with every Phonon model, but sounds best in its own language.
